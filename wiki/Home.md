# Claudian Starter: Wiki

This wiki is the living guide for the Claudian Starter. It grows as the project
grows. Every page links to the next, the way an Obsidian vault is meant to work.

Claudian is a **project and a tool**, not a product. It gives an AI helper a set
of rules so it can safely tidy, link, and back up your notes.

## Start here

- [[Getting Started]]: for people who only use Word, Excel, and folders.
- [[The Rules (Simple English)]]: what the AI helper may and may not do, in plain English.
- [[Glossary]]: every technical word, in one line each.

## How it keeps your notes safe

- [[Git Backup]]: how the tool saves a copy of everything, every hour.
- [[Sensitivity and Security]]: how private files are kept out of the backup, and what the safety check does before a save.
